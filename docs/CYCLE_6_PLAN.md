## Hypothesis and fixed scope

An investment decision can improve by comparing buy-now with buying after the **next** observed shop opening, and by charging price pressure from plausible additional rival production. Implement a short scenario tree rather than choosing with knowledge of a sampled complete future. Actual opening, hiring, dispatch, fertilizer/harvest timing, sales, land admission and candidate geometry remain frozen. Only expansion valuation/selection and its pure forecast helpers change.

- Eight equally weighted next-shop branches, using the pinned uniform-with-replacement shop law. Existing shops persist. Next opening follows the configured day interval and eight-instance cap. After that event, hold shop demand fixed in this short-lookahead approximation; later shops are deliberately unmodeled, not declared impossible. No game seed or future replay observations enter the agent.
- Within every next-shop branch, compare visible rival production with a bounded growth stress. The stress buys up to four plots of the rival's most common visible supported crop, then one of its most common visible animals if affordable. Use only currently vacant/weed cells on unlocked land, public cash less 150, and valid remaining-season yields. Do not assume an unseen species, new rival land, or unlimited capital. Charge this hypothetical herd's feed demand in the market; never charge its acquisitions to our farm.
- For each existing immediate option and its delayed version, project full dated cash under both supply cases. Use the smaller **paired marginal value** within each shop branch. Average those branch values for immediate purchases. Preserve the 150-coin pathwise reserve and route limits. Keep the existing family-per-column then total-batch ranking within the admitted menu.
- A delayed option buys on the next opening day and installs one day later, shifting seed/animal/land costs, yields, feed and service together. It can use forecast intervening receipts only if the cash path covers all spending before receipts. It may choose a different investment in each *observed next-shop branch*, but cannot choose separately by an unobserved rival stress or later shop path. No purchase has marginal value zero. Wait only when the resulting branch-average value exceeds the selected immediate value; replan on every real observation. No hard waiting latch or guessed future state is persisted.
- Reuse the current production accounting. Verify unchanged default forecasts, source scope, dated land costs, additional rival output/feed accounting, branchwise nonanticipativity, horizon/cash constraints and purity. Use local price memoization inside forecasts only if needed to keep the standalone agent within the one-second budget; do not change prices or policy semantics.

These are approximate one-purchase continuations, not a calibrated probability model of rivals or an optimal multi-stage farm plan. Waiting omits alternative intervening purchases and future dispatch may differ. Those limitations must be reported with the experiment.
