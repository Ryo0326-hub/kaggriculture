# Step 8: profitable land with executable daily routes

The Step 7 server games are complementary evidence: MugaBros won by expanding
profitable strawberry production; Jaikrishna@007 lost despite buying all land,
with costly feed, overflow, and unused seeds. Expansion is an investment whose
incremental terminal receipts must cover land, inputs, wages, and delivery.

Preserve `baselines/step_7.py` byte for byte. Keep its tested Day 1 opening and
installation, then compare bounded production batches on owned land and the next
quadrant. Enumerate integer alternatives, reprice the entire portfolio, preserve
dated cash feasibility, and require a daily route cover before buying. No land
salvage value or unseen future shops may enter the objective.

Use deterministic insertion tours with explicit service, pickup, movement, and
deposit allowances. Share a worker across nearby animals and crops. Keep hard
inventory reservations and terminal delivery deadlines. This is a bounded
vehicle-routing heuristic, not an exact solution of the whole farm problem.

Build an independently coded expanding mixed-farm opponent. It must react to
the current observation; replay action lists are accounting evidence only. Test
the candidate against that opponent, Step 7, and historical production families.

Before fresh validation, freeze source, opponent hashes, seeds, and gates. Require
zero candidate execution errors, unplanned crop losses, escapes, missed feeding,
seed overrequests, or leftover goods/seeds; verify runtime and isolated packaging.
Require positive paired pool-score evidence and investigate any opponent-class
regression over five percentage points. Every failed fresh batch is retired.

Keep selective maintenance, expanded crop species, and advanced sale timing for
later checkpoints. Save a reconciled server analysis and CO implementation notes.
